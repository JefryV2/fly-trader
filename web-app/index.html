A quick guide to FlyTrader

Start with the left rail. The rest is telemetry you can explore later.

1
Choose your stocks

One ticker gives you the single-stock fly. Two or more switch to the portfolio fly, which allocates across the group on a monthly rebalance.

2
Load the session

Press Load selected stocks, then either start Auto-Pilot to let it trade, or Train 25 eps to improve the brain.

3
Check the result honestly

The Portfolio tab has the held-out backtest and the Combo Lab. Validation is for choosing; the test row is the unseen check.

Controls

Auto-Pilot
Repeats the fly's decision at the chosen speed. Click it again to pause.
Train 25 eps
Runs 25 learning episodes and updates the Evolution Lab.
Run 5 sims
Runs five simulated episodes and saves them for comparison.
Lab
Opens the plasticity, loss, and valence telemetry.
Combo Lab
Tests groups of 2–8 stocks. Up to 240 candidate baskets are scored; larger pools use a guided beam, and very large pools ask you to narrow the selection.
Manual trades
Advanced one-off buy/sell controls. They are disabled for portfolio sessions.

Numbers

Return
Total change in the simulated account.
Sharpe
Return per unit of risk. Higher is better; — means there is not enough history yet.
Drawdown
The worst peak-to-trough fall in the session.
Win rate
The share of closed trades that ended positive.
DA
Dopamine, the fly's reward signal. Positive means the recent trade was rewarded.

Why the test number matters

The fly is selected on validation data, so the winning validation number is optimistic by construction. The test row is scored once on data the selection never saw. When the Combo Lab can use unseen tickers, it says so; that is the strongest check.
